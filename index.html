How to convert a bank statement PDF to Excel

  1. Download your statement as a PDF from your bank's website or app.
  2. Drop it into the box above. Enter the password if the PDF is protected.
  3. Check the rows in the preview and fix anything that looks off.
  4. Download the Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file.

Questions

Is it safe to convert my bank statement here?

Yes. The conversion runs in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to a server, and nothing is stored.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Yes. Type the PDF password in the password box, then add the file.

Does it work with scanned statements?

It works with text-based PDFs downloaded from your bank. Scanned image PDFs need OCR first.

Which banks does it support?

It reads most text-based statements that list the date, description, debit, credit and balance. Layouts vary by bank, so check the preview before downloading.
